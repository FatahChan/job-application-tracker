# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 12 November 2026_

Job Application Tracker is a Chrome extension for keeping a list of your job applications.

## What the extension stores

The details you enter for each application: role, company, posting link, status, salary, notes and dates.

## Where it is stored

Only on your device, in Chrome's extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`). The extension has no server and no account. It does not send your data anywhere, and the developer cannot see it.

Uninstalling the extension deletes this data. Use **Export** first if you want a backup file.

## Job pages

When you click **Fill Form** on a LinkedIn job posting, the extension reads the role, company and page address from that page to fill the form. It reads nothing else and runs on no other sites.

## What the extension does not do

- No analytics, tracking or advertising
- No selling or sharing of data
- No remote code
